Test of parameters gain and CfdFraction.

Uniform energy sweeps up to 20MeV
cfd fraction runs were made with gain = 2
gain runs were made with cfdFraction = 0.5
A higher cfd fraction resulted in a much more linear energy-energy-spectrum.
Different gain values did not affect the shape of the spectrum noticeably.


The 49K run was made with cfdFraction = 0.9 and gain = 10


Another important parameter to be aware of is the distance from the source to the bars. It is assumed
to be 1m, but 1.05m is propably more accurate. Since this distance is also squared, when calculating
the energy, it should be determined accurately.
